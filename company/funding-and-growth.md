# Funding and Growth

Tatum Games has raised approximately **$785,000 in angel investment**.

## 2021 Investment

In 2021, Tatum Games secured a $600,000 investment commitment.

The capital was milestone-based.

- $200,000 was released initially in 2021.
- Tatum Games then completed the required milestones.
- The remaining $400,000 was released at the end of 2022.

The milestone structure matters because the funding was tied to execution.

During this period, MIKROS remained private and was tested with approximately 100 game developers and studios.

Their feedback informed the product before its April 2023 public launch.

## 2025 Investment

In 2025, Tatum Games raised an additional $185,000 from angel investors.

One of the participating angel investors was a top executive at Bethesda.

## Revenue

Tatum Games does not publicly disclose exact revenue.

The company is working toward seven-figure annual recurring revenue and continues to focus on sustainable growth, paying customers, and multiple revenue engines rather than vanity metrics.

## Capital Philosophy

Tatum Games has intentionally operated as a lean company.

The business is not built around a blitzscale-at-all-costs strategy.

Its long-term focus is on:

- control;
- ownership;
- durable infrastructure;
- multiple revenue engines;
- ecosystem leverage;
- and sustainable execution.
