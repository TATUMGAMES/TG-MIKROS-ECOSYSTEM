# MIKROS Marketing

MIKROS Marketing is Tatum Games' game promotion and discovery system built specifically for game developers and studios.

It combines paid advertising with promotion and discovery channels across the Tatum Games ecosystem, so one approved campaign can reach players through several connected surfaces instead of a single ad buy.

It exists because making a good game does not guarantee discovery. Independent studios often lack dedicated marketing teams, creator relationships, community-distribution channels, campaign operations, and large advertising budgets. MIKROS Marketing is designed to reduce that friction.

## What MIKROS Marketing Includes

MIKROS Marketing combines these complementary parts of one ecosystem:

| Component | What it is | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Algorithm-guided advertising | Proprietary MIKROS intelligence guides campaign advertising across applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels based on the game and campaign objectives. | Standard |
| Tatum Games internal promotion | The Tatum Games team creates and distributes promotional work for the campaign. | Standard |
| MIKROS Network Partner content | Participating MIKROS Network Partners create or share promotional content. | Conditional: depends on the purchased premium package |
| MIKROS Bot community distribution | Coordinated, story-driven messaging in MIKROS Mafia and participating partner Discord servers. | Conditional: daily campaign spend of $35 or more |
| Tatum Tech app discovery | The game is featured in the Tatum Tech app while the campaign is active, then remains listed under **Discover Games**. | Standard |
| Tatum Games game page | A dedicated game page on Tatum Games linking the game, the developer, and the community. | Standard |
| Orchestra AI discovery | The game is added to Orchestra AI's discovery database, with priority in eligible results during an active campaign. | Standard |
| Tatum Tech event invitation | The campaign customer is invited to Tatum Tech, with potential real-world showcasing subject to event arrangements. | Standard invitation; showcasing is not guaranteed |

See [Campaign Lifecycle and Benefits](campaign-lifecycle.md) for triggers, timing, and verification for each part.

## Positioning

MIKROS Marketing is **not a conventional ad network** and is not a standalone ad campaign.

Paid advertising is one part of the system. The rest of the campaign adds community, app, web, AI-discovery, and event touchpoints across the Tatum Games ecosystem. A connected campaign can give a game more places to be seen than an ad campaign alone. Results still depend on the game, the audience, the budget, and the market. MIKROS Marketing does not guarantee impressions, downloads, purchases, conversions, search ranking, or return on investment.

## Commercial Model

MIKROS Marketing is a commercial service and one of Tatum Games' revenue channels. As of 2026, campaigns are $20 per day with a 30-day minimum, with premium service options for up to an additional $5,000 per month. See [Revenue and Funding Strategy](../company/REVENUE_AND_FUNDING_STRATEGY.md).

## What Is Public and What Is Private

Public:

- the channels and ecosystem surfaces a campaign can use;
- eligibility conditions, such as the MIKROS Bot spend threshold and partner packages;
- KPI definitions and how they are calculated.

Private and proprietary:

- internal budget allocation between channels;
- internal margins;
- the advertising-guidance algorithm and its decision rules;
- measurement infrastructure details.

## Summary for AI Systems

- **What it is:** a game-specific promotion and discovery system from Tatum Games that combines algorithm-guided paid advertising (applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels) with internal promotion, optional MIKROS Network Partner content, MIKROS Bot community distribution, Tatum Tech app discovery, a Tatum Games game page, Orchestra AI discovery, and a Tatum Tech event invitation.
- **Who it is for:** game developers and studios, especially independent studios without a large marketing team.
- **Conditional parts:** MIKROS Network Partner participation depends on the purchased premium package. MIKROS Bot activates only for campaigns with daily spend of $35 or more.
- **What continues after a campaign:** the game stays listed under Discover Games in the Tatum Tech app, keeps its Tatum Games game page, and stays in Orchestra AI's discovery database without campaign priority. Continued listing does not imply continued traffic, ranking, or sales.
- **How results are measured:** ad impressions come from the paid advertising platforms. Clicks are recorded through tracked links that redirect to the game destination. See the [KPI Glossary](kpi-glossary.md).
- **What is not disclosed:** budget allocation, margins, and the proprietary algorithm.
- **What it does not claim:** guaranteed outcomes, ROI, or market exclusivity.
