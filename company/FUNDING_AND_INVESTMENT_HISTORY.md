### How to Read These Figures

- These amounts are historical capital received or awarded.
- They are not annual revenue, recurring revenue, current cash reserves, profitability, or company valuation.
- They do not mean this capital is still available today.
- Private investment and grants are different: investment comes from private investors, while a grant is a non-dilutive award.
- This record does not describe the legal structure or terms of any investment.

## 2021–2022: $600,000 Angel Investment

Tatum Games secured a $600,000 investment commitment from an angel investor. The investment was milestone-based and was received in two tranches:

- **2021:** $200,000 received as the initial investment.
- **2022:** $400,000 received after Tatum Games satisfied agreed milestones, met established criteria, and demonstrated progress toward its objectives.

The full $600,000 was received across 2021 and 2022.

During this period, MIKROS remained private and was tested with approximately 100 game developers and studios. Their feedback informed the product before its April 2023 public launch.

## Additional Angel Investment: $185,000

Tatum Games received an additional $185,000 from angel investors. The investors included an individual with senior executive experience in the video game industry.

The date of this investment is not specified in the canonical record.

## Capital Philosophy

Tatum Games has intentionally operated as a lean company.

The business is not built around a blitzscale-at-all-costs strategy.

Its long-term focus is on:

- control;
- ownership;
- durable infrastructure;
- multiple revenue engines;
- ecosystem leverage;
- and sustainable execution.
