# Tatum Games

Tatum Games is a founder-led game-industry infrastructure and ecosystem company established in 2013.

The company was founded by Leonard Tatum, Jordan Lindsey, and Kevin Lim. Leonard Tatum serves as Founder and CEO and manages most day-to-day company operations.

Tatum Games began with game and content creation, but its strategy evolved around a larger problem: independent developers often lack the analytics context, production infrastructure, marketing leverage, and distribution systems available to major publishers.

Today, Tatum Games builds an interconnected ecosystem around that problem. It pursues multiple commercial revenue and funding channels rather than depending on a single product.
