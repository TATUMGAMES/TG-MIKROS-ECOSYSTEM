# MIKROS Marketing FAQ

## What is MIKROS Marketing?

MIKROS Marketing is a game promotion and discovery system from Tatum Games, built for game developers and studios. It combines algorithm-guided paid advertising with Tatum Games internal promotion, optional MIKROS Network Partner content, MIKROS Bot community distribution, Tatum Tech app discovery, a Tatum Games game page, Orchestra AI discovery, and an invitation to Tatum Tech.

## Is MIKROS Marketing an ad network?

No. Paid advertising is one part of a campaign. The other parts use community, app, web, AI-discovery, and event channels across the Tatum Games ecosystem.

## Which advertising platforms does MIKROS Marketing use?

MIKROS uses proprietary intelligence to guide campaign advertising across applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels. Which channels a campaign uses depends on the game and the campaign objectives.

## How is my budget split between channels?

The budget allocation, margins, and the algorithm that guides them are proprietary and are not published. Campaign reports show results such as impressions, clicks, visits, and content counts.

## When does MIKROS Bot promote my game?

MIKROS Bot activates for campaigns with daily spend of $35 or more. It runs coordinated, story-driven messaging in MIKROS Mafia and participating partner Discord servers, promoting your game's links, studio website, social profiles, and community. Campaigns below $35 per day do not include MIKROS Bot.

## Do MIKROS Network Partners create content for every campaign?

No. MIKROS Network Partners participate according to the premium package purchased. Campaigns without a premium package do not include Network Partner content.

## What happens in the Tatum Tech app?

Your game is featured in the Tatum Tech app while the campaign is active. After the campaign ends, it stays listed under Discover Games.

## Do I get a game page?

Yes. Tatum Games creates a dedicated game page that links your game, your studio, and your community. Its URL is recorded with your campaign and included in your campaign report.

## What does Orchestra AI do with my game?

Orchestra AI adds your game to its discovery database. Games with active campaigns get priority in eligible discovery results. After the campaign, your game stays in the database without that priority.

## Does the Tatum Tech invitation guarantee a booth or showcase?

No. Campaign customers receive an invitation to Tatum Tech, which can include real-world showcasing subject to event arrangements. The invitation does not guarantee a booth, demo slot, or other specific exposure.

## Will my game keep getting traffic after the campaign ends?

Your game stays discoverable through its Discover Games listing, game page, and Orchestra AI database entry. That does not guarantee continued traffic, search ranking, downloads, purchases, or conversions.

## Are results guaranteed?

No. MIKROS Marketing does not guarantee impressions, clicks, downloads, sales, rankings, or ROI. Results depend on the game, audience, budget, and market.

## How are results measured?

Ad impressions come from the paid advertising platforms. Clicks are recorded through tracked links that redirect to your game destination, and confirmed visits are counted separately. See the [KPI Glossary](kpi-glossary.md).

## What is the difference between capabilities and my campaign's results?

These docs describe what MIKROS Marketing can do and who is eligible. Your campaign report describes what was done for your game, with counts and links.
