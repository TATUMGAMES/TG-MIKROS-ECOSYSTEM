# MIKROS Marketing

MIKROS Marketing is Tatum Games' outcome-driven marketing platform built specifically for game developers and studios.

It exists because making a good game does not guarantee discovery.

Independent studios often lack:

- dedicated marketing teams;
- creator relationships;
- influencer infrastructure;
- community-distribution channels;
- campaign operations;
- and large advertising budgets.

MIKROS Marketing is designed to reduce that friction.

## What It Does

MIKROS Marketing helps developers launch campaigns that can activate:

- creators;
- influencers;
- writers;
- community channels;
- game-discovery partners;
- and other visibility opportunities.

Campaign performance can be tracked using mechanisms such as UTMs and verified clicks.

## Positioning

MIKROS Marketing is **not positioned as a conventional ad network**.

Its focus is helping games gain visibility and traction through real distribution activity.
