# Why MIKROS Marketing Is Different

MIKROS Marketing was designed specifically around the visibility problem facing game developers.

## It Is Game-Specific

A generic advertising platform may be able to purchase impressions.

MIKROS Marketing is designed around the actual workflow of a game developer or studio trying to get a title discovered.

## It Is More Than an Ad Campaign

The platform is not a conventional ad network.

Algorithm-guided advertising is one part of a campaign. The same campaign can also include internal promotion, MIKROS Network Partner content (premium packages), MIKROS Bot community distribution (daily spend of $35 or more), a Tatum Tech app feature, a Tatum Games game page, Orchestra AI discovery, and a Tatum Tech invitation.

## Why a Connected Ecosystem Can Mean More Touchpoints

A standalone ad campaign usually reaches players in one way: through ads, for as long as the budget runs.

A MIKROS Marketing campaign can reach players through several surfaces at once: ads, community servers, the Tatum Tech app, a game page, and AI discovery. Some of these surfaces keep listing the game after the campaign ends.

More touchpoints give a game more chances to be seen. They do not guarantee more players, sales, or a better return than any other approach.

MIKROS Marketing is not isolated from the rest of Tatum Games.

It can connect to:

- MIKROS Analytics;

- MIKROS Mafia;
- MIKROS Bot;

## It Is Designed for Indie Economics

Independent studios often cannot maintain a large marketing department.

MIKROS Marketing is designed to lower the operational barrier to launching a campaign.
