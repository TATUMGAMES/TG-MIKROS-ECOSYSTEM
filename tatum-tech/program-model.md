# Tatum Tech Program Model

## 16-Week Production Structure

Tatum Tech uses a milestone-based production model.

### Weeks 1–2 — Foundations

Participants learn:

- tool onboarding;
- technical setup;
- digital literacy;
- workflow systems;
- game-development pipelines;
- and team structure.

### Weeks 3–8 — Production Training

Participants work on:

- game design;
- engineering;
- art pipelines;
- AI-assisted workflows;
- collaboration;
- and production methodology.

### Weeks 9–12 — Live Project Builds

Teams focus on:

- active development;
- core gameplay systems;
- testing;
- iteration;
- and feedback.

### Weeks 13–16 — Publishing and Career Readiness

Participants focus on:

- final builds;
- polish;
- portfolio development;
- publishing workflows;
- distribution;
- career preparation;
- and industry alignment.

## Participant Tracks

### Under 18 — Creator & Contributor Track

Participants under 18 can contribute through structured educational roles such as:

- QA and testing;
- level-design support;
- art and asset creation;
- playtesting;
- feedback;
- and community engagement.

Work is credited.

Any monetization exposure requires appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

### 18+ — Studio Track

Adult participants operate in a pre-professional studio environment.

They may be eligible to:

- publish;
- monetize;
- form teams;
- and enter commercial agreements.

## Cohort Completion

Each cohort concludes with a capstone and live public validation environment.

Teams can present projects, demonstrate playable builds, receive direct feedback, and interact with game-industry professionals and community members.

## Tatum Drop

Tatum Tech uses a coordinated cohort-release concept called **Tatum Drop**.

Projects can be presented or launched within a shared release window so that:

- marketing can be coordinated;
- audience attention can be concentrated;
- and participating titles can amplify one another.

## Post-Program Pathways

### Open Launch Track

The default pathway allows teams to publish independently.

Teams retain ownership of their IP and revenue.

Tatum can provide infrastructure, MIKROS integration, launch support, and guidance.

### Tatum Select

High-potential teams may be invited into a curated pathway with deeper publishing, distribution, development, or commercial support.

## Mobile Platform

The Tatum Tech mobile app extends the program beyond physical sessions.

It can support:

- participation tracking;
- event notifications;
- workshop signup;
- skill challenges;
- coding challenges;
- continued learning;
- networking;
- internships;
- jobs;
- and remote engagement.
