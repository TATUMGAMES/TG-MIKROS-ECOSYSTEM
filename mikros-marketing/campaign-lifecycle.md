# MIKROS Marketing Campaign Lifecycle and Benefits

This page describes what happens after a MIKROS Marketing campaign is approved, which parts are conditional, and what continues after the campaign ends.

It describes how the system works. It does not describe the results of any specific campaign. A campaign's own report states what was actually done for that game.

## Lifecycle

### 1. Campaign work begins

After approval, the Tatum Games team begins campaign work, including internal promotional content. MIKROS Network Partners participate according to the premium package purchased. Campaigns without a premium package do not include Network Partner content.

Paid advertising runs across the applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels, guided by proprietary MIKROS intelligence.

### 2. MIKROS Bot community distribution (spend threshold)

Campaigns with **daily spend of $35 or more** activate MIKROS Bot. MIKROS Bot runs coordinated, story-driven messaging in [MIKROS Mafia](../mikros-mafia/README.md) and participating partner Discord servers. The messaging promotes the game's links, the studio's website, its social profiles, and its community.

Campaigns below $35 per day do not activate MIKROS Bot.

### 3. Tatum Tech app feature and Discover Games listing

While the campaign is active, the game is featured in the Tatum Tech app. After the campaign ends, the game stays listed under **Discover Games** in the app.

### 4. Tatum Games game page

Tatum Games creates a dedicated game page for the campaign's game. The page links to the game, the developer, and the developer's community. Each game page has its own address. The campaign record holds the exact URL.

### 5. Orchestra AI discovery

Orchestra AI adds the game to its discovery database. Games with active campaigns get priority in eligible discovery results. After the priority period ends, the game stays in the database.

### 6. Tatum Tech invitation

The campaign customer receives an invitation to [Tatum Tech](../tatum-tech/README.md). That can include real-world showcasing of the game, subject to event arrangements. The invitation does not guarantee a booth, a demo slot, a speaking opportunity, or any specific exposure.

## Benefits Table

| Benefit | Trigger | During the campaign | After the campaign | How it is verified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Algorithm-guided advertising | Campaign approval | Ads run on applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels | Ends with the campaign | Impressions from the advertising platforms; recorded clicks through tracked links |
| Internal promotion | Campaign approval | The Tatum Games team creates and distributes promotional content | Published content may remain online; no new content is created | Count of qualifying content items and links to them |
| MIKROS Network Partner content | Purchased premium package | Participating partners create or share content according to the package | Published content may remain online | Count of qualifying partner content items and links to them |
| MIKROS Bot distribution | Daily spend of $35 or more | Story-driven messaging in MIKROS Mafia and participating partner Discord servers | Ends with the campaign | Bot activity summary and community links |
| Tatum Tech app | Campaign approval | Featured placement in the app | Listed under Discover Games | Game visible in the app |
| Tatum Games game page | Campaign approval | Dedicated page links the game, developer, and community | Page remains | Live game page URL from the campaign record |
| Orchestra AI discovery | Campaign approval | Priority in eligible discovery results | Stays in the discovery database without priority | Inclusion in the Orchestra AI discovery database |
| Tatum Tech invitation | Campaign approval | Invitation to Tatum Tech | Showcasing depends on event arrangements | Invitation details sent to the customer |

## What Continued Discovery Means

The Discover Games listing, game page, and Orchestra AI database entry keep a game discoverable after its campaign ends. Continued listing does not mean continued traffic, search ranking, downloads, purchases, or conversions.
