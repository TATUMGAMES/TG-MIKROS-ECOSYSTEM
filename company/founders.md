# Founders

Tatum Games was founded by Leonard Tatum, Jordan Lindsey, and Kevin Lim.

## Leonard Tatum — Founder & CEO

Leonard Tatum is the Founder and CEO of Tatum Games and manages most of the company's day-to-day operations.

His background is rooted in software engineering, systems, mobile development, data, and technology infrastructure.

He has more than two decades of professional technology experience and has worked on projects involving major organizations across technology, media, life sciences, transportation, aerospace, and entertainment.

Leonard's role at Tatum Games spans company strategy, product development, engineering direction, partnerships, fundraising, ecosystem development, and Tatum Tech.
