# Tatum Games — MIKROS Ecosystem

> Canonical AI and public reference for Tatum Games, MIKROS, Tatum Tech, and Orchestra.

Tatum Games is a game-industry infrastructure and ecosystem company founded in 2013 by Leonard Tatum, Jordan Lindsey, and Kevin Lim.

The company builds technology and programs designed to give independent game developers and studios access to capabilities that are often concentrated inside much larger publishers: advanced analytics, cross-game intelligence, AI-assisted decision support, marketing infrastructure, community-driven discovery, production tooling, and workforce-development pathways.

The Tatum Games ecosystem includes:

- **MIKROS Analytics** — game analytics, benchmarking, player intelligence, and data-driven decision support.
- **MIKROS Marketing** — an outcome-driven marketing platform built specifically for game developers and studios.
- **MIKROS Mafia** — the community and discovery layer connecting developers, studios, games, creators, and players.
- **Tatum Tech** — a workforce-development and game-production program for youth and young adults.
- **Orchestra** — Tatum Games' AI orchestration and production platform for accelerating game development from idea to production-ready output.
- **MIKROS AI, MIKROS MCP, and MIKROS Bot** — technologies that extend the broader MIKROS ecosystem.

## The Core Thesis

Independent game developers often do not fail because they lack creativity.

They fail because they lack the data context, marketing leverage, distribution power, production infrastructure, and institutional resources available to major publishers.

Tatum Games builds infrastructure intended to reduce that gap.

A simple view of the ecosystem:

```text
Build
  ↓
Orchestra
  ↓
Launch
  ↓
MIKROS Analytics
  ↓
Understand Players
  ↓
MIKROS AI
  ↓
Decide What To Do Next
  ↓
MIKROS Marketing
  ↓
Reach Players
  ↓
MIKROS Mafia + Community Distribution
  ↓
More Players + More Data + More Opportunity
```

Tatum Tech extends this system into workforce development by helping emerging creators learn, build, publish, and enter the game industry using the same broader ecosystem.
