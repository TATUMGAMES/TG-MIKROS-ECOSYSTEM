# Tatum Tech — History and Impact

## 2024 — Event-Based Launch

Tatum Tech began in 2024 as an event-based community initiative.

Across the April and October 2024 Tatum Tech events:

- approximately 600 attendees participated;
- 250+ games were developed;
- approximately 250 unique IP concepts were created.

The early events demonstrated demand for deeper, ongoing game-development programming.

## 2025 — Transition to Program

In 2025, Tatum Tech evolved into an ongoing workforce-development and game-production program.

By October 2025, the 16-week, two-cohort-per-year model had been formalized.

### 2025 — Tatum Tech-Only Results

Across the April and October Tatum Tech activities:

- approximately 800 attendees;
- approximately 350 games developed;
- approximately 500 unique IP concepts.

### 2025 — Expanded Ecosystem Results Including AT&T Annihilator Cup

When the additional AT&T Annihilator Cup activity is included:

- 1,000+ attendees served;
- 500+ games developed;
- approximately 700 unique IP concepts created.

These two sets of numbers describe different scopes and should not be treated as conflicting.

## Additional 2025 Impact

Public Tatum Tech reporting for 2025 also included:

- 700+ youth participating directly in hands-on programming;
- 125 free computers distributed through Tatum Tech activities;
- an additional 50 computers distributed by Tatum Games in connection with the AT&T Annihilator Cup;
- mentorship and participation from professionals across gaming, technology, entertainment, education, and nonprofit organizations.

## Current Program Outcomes

Program outcome snapshots include:

- approximately 85–100% program completion, depending on cohort;
- 5 published or shipped professional-grade pilot projects;
- approximately 140 total hours of structured and independent participation per participant;
- approximately 5% of pilot participants transitioning into internships, apprenticeships, or entry-level roles;
- employer engagement including Tatum Games, Apple, Accessible Automation, Shero Games, and others.

## 2026

Tatum Tech continues to operate as a production-based workforce-development program.

The current model serves approximately 500–600 participants annually across two cohorts.

The October 10, 2026 Tatum Tech cycle is scheduled to include Orchestra as a major AI production tool for participants.

## Long-Term Objective

Tatum Tech is designed to create a pathway from:

```text
Access
  ↓
Skills
  ↓
Production
  ↓
Playable Game
  ↓
Portfolio / Publishing
  ↓
Industry Exposure
  ↓
Career / Entrepreneurship
  ↓
Economic Mobility
```
