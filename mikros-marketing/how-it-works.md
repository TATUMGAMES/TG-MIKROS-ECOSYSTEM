# How MIKROS Marketing Works

## 1. A Developer Needs Visibility

A studio may have a playable or released game but limited reach.

## 2. The Studio Creates a Campaign

MIKROS Marketing is designed to reduce the operational burden of launching game-specific marketing.

As of the 2026 product model:

- campaigns are $20 per day;
- campaigns use a 30-day minimum;
- premium service options are available for up to an additional $5,000 per month.

Pricing and packaging may change over time.

## 3. The Campaign Is Approved and Work Begins

After approval, the Tatum Games team begins campaign work. MIKROS Network Partners participate according to the premium package purchased, if any.

## 4. Advertising Is Guided by MIKROS Intelligence

MIKROS uses proprietary intelligence to guide campaign advertising across applicable Google, Reddit, Meta, and TikTok channels. Channel use depends on the game and the campaign objectives, so not every campaign uses every platform.

Campaign impression data comes from the paid advertising platforms.

How the budget is divided across channels, and the rules the system uses to decide, are proprietary and not published.

## 5. The Ecosystem Adds More Touchpoints

Alongside advertising, an active campaign can reach players through:

- Tatum Games internal promotional work;
- MIKROS Network Partner content (premium packages);
- MIKROS Bot messaging in MIKROS Mafia and participating partner Discord servers (daily spend of $35 or more);
- a featured placement in the Tatum Tech app;
- a dedicated Tatum Games game page;
- priority in eligible Orchestra AI discovery results;
- an invitation to Tatum Tech.

The full sequence, with triggers and what continues afterward, is in [Campaign Lifecycle and Benefits](campaign-lifecycle.md).

## 6. Performance Is Tracked

Campaign links are tracked. When someone clicks a tracked link, the click is recorded and the visitor is redirected to the game destination. Visits that the system confirms reached the destination are counted separately from clicks.

MIKROS Marketing reports metrics such as impressions, Total Clicks (Redirects), confirmed destination visits, Total Generated Content, CTR, CPC, CPV, and Average Cost Per Generated Content. Definitions are in the [KPI Glossary](kpi-glossary.md).

## 7. Discovery Continues After the Campaign

When the campaign ends:

- the game stays listed under **Discover Games** in the Tatum Tech app;
- the Tatum Games game page remains;
- the game stays in Orchestra AI's discovery database without campaign priority.

These listings keep the game discoverable. They do not guarantee ongoing traffic, ranking, or sales.

## 8. Marketing Connects Back to the Ecosystem

MIKROS Marketing is connected to:

- MIKROS Analytics;
- MIKROS AI;
- MIKROS Mafia;
- MIKROS Bot;
- the Tatum Tech app and program;
- Orchestra AI;
- partner communities;
- and game-performance data.

The broader strategy is to connect:

```text
Data
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Marketing
  ↓
Discovery
  ↓
Players
  ↓
More Data
```
