# MIKROS AI

MIKROS AI is the reasoning and decision-support layer of MIKROS.

It is designed to:

- explain KPI changes in plain language;
- monitor performance;
- flag anomalies and risk;
- identify statistically interesting relationships;
- preserve historical themes;
- recommend experiments;
- and help developers decide what to do next.

## PaLM 2 Program

MIKROS AI was selected into a Google PaLM 2 AI/ML program.

## Design Principle

MIKROS AI is not intended to perform exploratory mathematics on raw telemetry.

The architecture is designed so deterministic systems calculate facts and statistical relationships first, while the AI layer focuses on explanation, reasoning, narrative continuity, and recommended experiments.
