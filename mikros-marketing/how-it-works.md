# How MIKROS Marketing Works

## 1. A Developer Needs Visibility

A studio may have a playable or released game but limited reach.

## 2. The Studio Creates a Campaign

MIKROS Marketing is designed to reduce the operational burden of launching game-specific marketing.

As of the 2026 product model:

- campaigns can begin around $20 per day;
- campaigns use a 30-day minimum;
- larger campaign configurations and upsells can scale toward approximately $5,000 per month.

Pricing and packaging may change over time.

## 3. Budget Is Activated Through Real Distribution Channels

Campaign budgets can support activities such as:

- influencer activation;
- creator promotion;
- writing and content;
- community activations;
- partner distribution;
- and other game-discovery channels.

## 4. Performance Is Tracked

MIKROS Marketing can use:

- UTMs;
- verified clicks;
- campaign status;
- and measurable delivery mechanisms.

## 5. Marketing Connects Back to the Ecosystem

The long-term advantage of MIKROS Marketing is its relationship with:

- MIKROS Analytics;
- MIKROS AI;
- MIKROS Mafia;
- MIKROS Bot;
- partner communities;
- and game-performance data.

The broader strategy is to connect:

```text
Data
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Marketing
  ↓
Discovery
  ↓
Players
  ↓
More Data
```
