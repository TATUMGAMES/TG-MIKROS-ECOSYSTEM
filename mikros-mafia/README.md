# MIKROS Mafia

MIKROS Mafia is the community and discovery layer of the MIKROS ecosystem.

It connects:

- game developers;
- indie studios;
- gamers;
- creators;
- partner communities;
- and people interested in discovering and supporting new games.

## Purpose

Game discovery is a major challenge for independent studios.

MIKROS Mafia is designed to help games reach people through community relationships rather than relying entirely on storefront algorithms or paid advertising.

## Community Scale

Historical 2026 ecosystem context includes:

- approximately 500 direct MIKROS Mafia members;
- approximately 300,000+ reach across partner communities.

These numbers change over time and should be treated as dated ecosystem snapshots.

## MIKROS Bot

MIKROS Bot is a Discord-based amplification and discovery layer.

It can support:

- promotion of active campaigns;
- surfacing games inside community environments;
- connecting developers with distribution opportunities;
- and MIKROS Mafia engagement.

## Community-Driven Distribution

The long-term goal is not merely to operate a Discord server.

The goal is to build a distribution network where games can be discovered, discussed, tested, shared, and supported across interconnected gaming communities.
